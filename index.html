About Her

Bushra Rahman

B

There are people who enter your life quietly, without needing attention — and yet, from the moment they arrive, everything feels different. Bushra.... my baby… is that person for me. She didn’t come into my life loudly, but she changed everything within me in the most gentle and beautiful way.

She carries a rare kind of beauty — not just in how she looks, but in who she is. Her modesty, her faith, and the way she carries herself with dignity make her stand apart in a world that often forgets these values. She is a God-fearing soul, someone who chooses what is right, even when it’s difficult. And that alone makes her more beautiful than anything words can describe.

She is kind in the purest way. She cares deeply, loves honestly, and stays loyal in a way that makes everything feel safe. She is not like others — she is real, grounded, and genuine. There is a softness in her heart, but also a quiet strength that I admire more than anything.

I don’t just love her for her best days, her smiles, or her happiness. I love her on her hardest days, when she feels low, when she feels tired, when the world feels heavy. Because she deserves a love that stays — always.

She is my peace in chaos, my clarity in confusion, and the reason I want to become better every single day. Loving her feels pure, real, and right — like something that was always meant to be.

Our Story

How It All Began

🌸

How We Met

I met Bushra on Instagram when we were in class 11. It started with something simple — I replied to one of her stories. From that day, we began talking, and slowly those small conversations turned into something meaningful. Somewhere along the way, I started feeling that there was something special about us — something different, something real. And then on 23rd January, I finally told her how I felt. That day wasn’t just a confession… it was the beginning of us.

🕯️

Growing Closer

What we had in the beginning was soft and fragile, something new and delicate. But with every passing day, we grew stronger together. There was a time when a third person tried to create misunderstandings between us, trying to shake what we were building. And for a moment, things felt uncertain. But the truth is, what we have was always stronger than outside noise. Instead of breaking us, it brought us closer. We understood each other better, trusted each other more, and chose to stand together. Now, we’re happier and stronger than ever—because in the end, no one could come between what’s real.

❤️

Memories Together

When we finally met, it felt quiet… but special in a way words can’t fully explain. Being next to you, seeing you in real life—it was a moment I’ll always carry with me. Recently, we watched a movie together, and honestly, it became one of my favorite memories. Not because of the movie itself—I don’t even remember all of it—but because of you. My whole attention was on you, on every little moment we shared. The way you fed me popcorn and ice cream with your hands… it was such a small thing, but it meant so much to me. Sometimes, it’s not the big moments that stay forever— it’s these simple, quiet memories with you that I’ll never forget.

Love Letter

To My Pretty lady

💌

My dearest Bushra,

I don’t think I’ll ever be able to fully explain how much I love you, but I try—through everything I do. Every effort I make, every moment I give, every thought I carry… it all comes from one place—my love for you. It’s real, it’s deep, and it’s something I’ve never felt like this before.

I trust you with my whole heart. That trust means everything to me. It’s what makes us strong, what makes us us. And that’s why, when we have arguments or when things get intense, and harsh words come out… it hurts me more than I can show. Not because I’m weak, but because what we have matters so much to me.

I never want us to hurt each other like that. I want us to understand each other, to be patient, to protect what we have instead of letting small moments take over something so big.

Because at the end of the day, it’s you. It has always been you. I don’t just love you for the good times—I love you in every moment, even the imperfect ones.

I choose you, always. No matter what.

Not from a Lover but from a Longer,

Hamza Ahmed 💗
